# Privacy And Consent Draft

This is starter wording for the Largs Colts 2016s app. It should be reviewed by the club before real parent rollout.

## Purpose

The app is used to manage Largs Colts 2016s team activity, including fixtures, training, availability, attendance, collection status, coach messages and parent notifications.

## Data Stored

The app may store:

- Child name
- Team colour
- Fixture and training attendance
- Availability responses
- Parent or guardian account details
- Parent-child approval links
- Coach messages
- Push notification tokens
- Notification history

## Access

Coaches can view all teams.

Parents can only access child information after a coach verifies and approves the parent-child link.

## Notifications

Parents may receive push notifications for important team updates, including when a child is marked present or collected.

## Consent

By using the app, the parent or guardian confirms that:

- They are allowed to request access for the selected child.
- The club may store the information needed to manage team activity.
- The club may send push notifications about fixtures, training, attendance and collection.
- They will contact the club if access should be removed.
